01 — The opportunity

Where does the extra money come from?

When a property is sold to satisfy a debt, the sale proceeds pay off what's owed first. If anything is left after those obligations are satisfied, it's considered surplus — sometimes called excess proceeds.

That money doesn't automatically go back. Someone has to claim it — before a deadline set by law.

  1. 01
    Property is soldAt a foreclosure auction or a tax sale.
  2. 02
    Debts and costs are paidMortgage or taxes, liens, legal and sale fees.
  3. 03
    Anything remaining is heldOften by the court registry or county — until claimed.
